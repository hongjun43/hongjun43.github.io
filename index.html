I’m Hong Jun Lee, a first‑year PhD student in Computer Science at King’s College London.

I work on Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning and Large Language Model Agents: Agents learning to learn together, what update rules they should follow, how they adapt to each other, and how they should reason.

Email GitHub

About

I started my PhD in June 2026, supervised by Stefanos Leonardos and Yali Du. Before the PhD, I spent two years working as an Actuary and a Data Scientist in London.

My research asks how learning rules should change when other agents are also learning: Meta-Gradients, Self-Play/Co-Evolve, LLM Agents.

I speak English, Mandarin and Malay. To a lesser extent: Japanese, Cantonese and Hokkien. Away from the desk: chess, video games, reading.

Research interests

What I’m interested in and am working on.

01

Learned multi-agent update rules

Meta-gradient learning in a multi agent setting, where every agent is discovering update rules in the same environment, rather than fixing it by hand./p>

02

Strategic adaptation to other learners

Agents that treat co-players as learners rather than part of the environment, and adapt to, anticipate, and shape how they learn.

03

Self-play/Co-evolve

Designing curriculums that grow together with the solver for recursive self-improvement.

03

Strategic LLM agents that reason about other agents

Language-model agents that improve on strategic reasoning capabilities, safety, and alignment.
