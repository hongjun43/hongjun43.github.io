About

I started my PhD in June 2026, supervised by Stefanos Leonardos and Yali Du in the Distributed Artificial Intelligence group at King’s.

I obtained my MSc from the London School of Economics (LSE), and spent two years working as an actuary and a data scientist in London.

Away from the desk, I enjoy chess, video games and reading. I am native in English, Mandarin and Malay.

My Erdős number is 4.

Research interests

  • Learned multi-agent update rules

    How can meta-learning help discover update rules for agents that are learning together?

  • Strategic adaptation to other learners

    How can agents anticipate, adapt to, and shape the learning of the agents they interact with?

  • Self-play and co-evolution

    How can opponents, curriculums, and tasks evolve alongside an agent to sustain useful learning?

  • Strategic LLM agents that reason about other agents

    How can language-model agents reason about others' behaviour, and use it to understand strategic interactions?
